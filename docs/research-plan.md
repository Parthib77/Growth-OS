# Salon-owner research plan

## Research question

Can a salon owner use Growth OS during a busy day to identify the right follow-up, respect contact permission, hand off a personal message, record a booking, and understand the resulting report without CRM training?

This document plans research. It does not report completed interviews or findings.

## Participants

Recruit 6 to 8 owners or managers of appointment-based businesses. At least 4 participants should work in salons. Include a mix of solo operators and businesses with a small team. Include participants who receive enquiries through WhatsApp or Instagram and participants who currently use a spreadsheet or paper notes.

Exclude product staff, CRM consultants, and participants who do not handle enquiries or review the resulting work.

Record each participant's business type, team size, enquiry channels, weekly enquiry range, current tracking method, primary device, and consent practice. Do not collect customer names, phone numbers, or message contents.

## Interview outline

Use a 45-minute session.

1. Ask the participant to describe the last real enquiry from arrival to booking or loss.
2. Ask where follow-up reminders, consent evidence, quoted price, and booking outcome live today.
3. Ask how the participant chooses whom to contact first.
4. Ask what makes a follow-up inappropriate or risky.
5. Ask how the participant knows whether follow-up work led to a booking.
6. Ask what the participant needs to see on a phone during a busy day.
7. Ask which data the participant would expect to export or delete.

Do not ask leading questions about planned features. Use the participant's words in research notes when permission allows.

## Usability tasks

Use a seeded demo workspace with fictional data. Ask the participant to think aloud.

1. Register and complete business setup.
2. Add an enquiry from a phone call and preserve the quoted value and permission state.
3. Import a short CSV and resolve a possible duplicate without merging it.
4. Use Today to choose the next enquiry and explain why it appears.
5. Create a campaign, identify an excluded recipient, and review a personalized message.
6. Open the WhatsApp action and return to record the outcome.
7. Record a booking and find the booking in Results.
8. Explain the difference between quoted value and recorded booking value.
9. Draft and copy a response to an unanswered review.
10. Export data and locate the account-deletion controls.

For Today and campaign review, test the selected operations-register composition. Ask participants to compare several records, open the detail inspector, return to the register, and continue without losing place. On phones, test the full-height record dialog and focus return.

Run the complete set on a phone for at least half of the sessions. Run the remaining sessions on the participant's usual device.

## Observation measures

For each task, record completion, recoverable errors, unrecovered errors, time on task, requests for help, and the participant's explanation of the result. Record whether the participant notices consent, exclusion reasons, saved state, attribution, and destructive-action confirmation.

After the tasks, ask the participant to rate confidence on a 1 to 5 scale and explain the rating. The explanation matters more than the number.

## Decision rules

- If fewer than 5 of 6 first-round participants can explain a Today priority reason without help, revise the rule explanation before adding more rules.
- If any participant expects a click-to-chat action to prove delivery, revise the labels before release.
- If more than one participant records quoted value as collected revenue, revise Results terminology and information order.
- If more than one phone participant cannot complete a core task, treat the mobile layout as a release blocker.
- If fewer than 5 of 6 first-round participants can compare two Today records without losing their place, revise the register and inspector relationship.
- If participants disagree on consent meaning, obtain legal and domain review before changing eligibility rules.

These thresholds guide iteration. They do not establish market fit or business impact.

## Evidence handling

Obtain consent for notes or recording before the session. Assign participant codes. Store the recruitment record separately from session notes. Remove customer data if a participant shares it by mistake. Limit access to the research team and delete recordings according to the approved research-retention policy.

Save the script, anonymized notes, task results, and design changes under a dated research folder. Add completed evidence to the case study only after review. Keep open questions labeled as open.

## Research sequence

1. Conduct two workflow interviews before finalizing seeded demo scenarios.
2. Run two formative usability sessions on the vertical slice.
3. Fix repeated comprehension and mobile failures.
4. Run four to six end-to-end sessions before release review.
5. Revisit the workflow after four weeks of real use if production access and consent exist.
